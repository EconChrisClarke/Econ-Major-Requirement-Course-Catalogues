Does your WSU major require economics? A 13-year picture

Every WSU bachelor's major, sorted into six categories by how ECONS 101/102 shows up in its degree requirements — merged with what fills the UCORE Social Science slot for majors that don't use econs. Weighted by each year's actual bachelor's degrees awarded (IPEDS), not just by major count. The outer ring groups the six into a binary split: Econ (101/102 required or offered as an option) vs. No Econ (a different course fills the slot, or it's genuinely open). Full primary-source catalog research, repeated independently for all 13 years 2013–2025.

Reading the six categories. The first three (blue/orange/aqua) come from each major's own published Schedule of Studies for ECONS 101/102 specifically. The last three (yellow/magenta/green) apply only to majors that don't mention econs at all — a second, independent research pass asks what non-econ course (if any) actually fills their UCORE Social Science slot, so "No Econ" isn't just a leftover bucket, it's its own researched breakdown.

Methodology & notes on data quality
  • Every year 2014–2024 (2013 and 2025 researched in an earlier pass) got the same full major-by-major catalog research as the anchor years — not an interpolation or a change-detection shortcut. Roughly 90 majors/options were checked per year against that year's actual printed catalog (rendered PDF pages plus pdftotext -layout extraction), across 77 research passes.
  • Sizes are annual bachelor's degrees awarded, from NCES IPEDS Completions data matched to each major via its CIP code (with a handful of majors' CIP classification drifting between adjacent codes across years — e.g. Economics reported under 45.0601 in most years but 45.0603 in others — both codes are summed for those majors). A major with no matched size data is excluded from that year's percentages, not counted as zero.
  • Some majors have multiple options/tracks that split between categories — e.g. within Mathematics & Statistics, the Actuarial Science option has required ECONS 101 & 102 flatly since at least 2017, while the Applied/Theoretical Math and Statistics options leave the slot open, and Secondary Teaching requires PSYCH 105. That whole major is coded Mixed rather than forced into one bucket.
  • A correction worth flagging as an example of what the re-research caught: in the 2016 catalog, all eight Carson College of Business majors were initially misread as requiring only one econ course, when the schedule in fact lists ECONS 101/102 as two separate first-year term slots (the "both" pattern seen in every adjacent year) — corrected after direct verification against the catalog text.
  • The Murrow College cluster (Strategic Communication + Journalism & Media Production) required a judgment call. COM 101 ("Media and Society") is a required College Core course for every Murrow major, but the catalog is internally inconsistent about whether it fills the UCORE Social Science [SSCI] slot: WSU's own course-description entry for COM 101 first carries an explicit [SSCI] tag starting in the 2015 catalog (it's untagged in 2013–2014), yet the Schedule of Studies itself never prints that tag next to COM 101 the way it does for every other double-duty course in the same schedule — and from 2015–2022 it additionally lists a separate "Social Sciences [SSCI]" line alongside (not fulfilled by) COM 101, later replaced (2023–2025) by a flexible "complete 5 of 6 UCORE designations" bundle that still doesn't name COM 101. On balance, Murrow is coded Requires a different course for every year 2015–2025, once COM 101 carries its catalog-of-record [SSCI] tag, and Open for 2013–2014, when it carried no UCORE tag at all.
  • The Murrow College's eight sub-tracks (Strategic Communication ×4, Journalism & Media Production ×4) are collapsed into one combined row each year, consistent across all 13 years, to avoid one college's internal restructuring from swinging the chart.
